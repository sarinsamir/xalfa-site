Hubs across India

Done in India

Work that mostly happens abroad today

XALFA plans to build aircraft lifecycle hubs across India. Our first proposal is to run the Saifai airstrip in Etawah, Uttar Pradesh. Each hub would be a single airfield for aircraft storage, inspection and teardown, with licensed maintenance partners on site. Doing this work in India would keep it in the country and bring skilled aviation jobs to Etawah and to each place a hub is built.

The opportunity

Aircraft work leaves India. We want to change that.

  • India's aircraft maintenance and repair work is mostly done abroad. A parliamentary committee reported in August 2025 that around 85% of MRO activity is done outside India.Source: The Tribune, 20 August 2025
  • Flying aircraft overseas for checks, storage or parting out adds ferry time and cost.
  • Bringing the work to well-placed airfields in India can shorten turnaround and keep value in the country.

What we plan to offer

One airfield, several kinds of aviation work

Each hub would be a single airfield with space for several kinds of aviation work. Regulated activity would be carried out by licensed partners.

01

Lease returns and storage

For lessors

Aircraft inspected, stored and prepared for their next operator in one place, with licensed maintenance companies on the same airfield.

02

Customs-bonded handling

For overseas owners and lessors

We plan to seek customs-bonded status for the site, so aircraft and parts can be stored and worked on with Indian customs duty deferred, and parts that leave India carry no Indian customs duty. The exact scope, including teardown, would be agreed with the customs authorities.

03

Heavy checks, parking and returns

For airlines

Hangar and apron space for heavy checks, long-term parking and lease-return handling.

04

Storage and teardown

For parts and teardown firms

A site for storage and teardown of retired aircraft, so good parts can be sourced and sold.

05

Maintenance sites

For MRO companies

Hangar space, utilities and a base on the same airfield where licensed maintenance companies would operate.

06

Spares, training and service

For aircraft and engine makers

Space for a spares and support depot, an authorised service site, or a training and test facility.

07

Flight testing and assembly

For drone and unmanned aircraft firms

A long runway for flight testing and trials, with hangar space for assembly, maintenance and repair. Airspace and approvals would be agreed with the authorities.

How it works

Who does what

XALFA

Would run the airfield, if the proposal succeeds.

Licensed partners

Would carry out regulated maintenance and teardown work.

Customers

Lessors, airlines, MROs, parts firms, OEMs and drone firms would use the site.

XALFA does not hold aviation licences itself. Maintenance and other regulated work would be done by organisations approved by the relevant authorities.

Questions

Who would do the maintenance work?

Licensed partners. XALFA does not hold aviation licences itself. Maintenance and other regulated work would be done by organisations approved by the relevant authorities.

What is the status?

We are preparing a proposal to run the Saifai airstrip. Nothing has been filed or awarded yet.

How would customs work?

We plan to seek customs-bonded status for the site. The exact scope, including teardown, would be agreed with the customs authorities.

Who would run the airfield?

XALFA would run the airfield if the proposal succeeds. Partners and customers would work on site.

Can we register interest?

Yes. If you would use a site like this, tell us what you would need. A short note helps our proposal and does not commit you.

Will XALFA only operate at Saifai?

No. Saifai is our first proposed site. The model is meant to be repeated at other airfields across India. Nothing has been filed or awarded yet.

What aircraft could the airfield handle?

The runway is 2,500 m long. Which aircraft types and weights the airfield could take depends on runway strength and approvals, which we would confirm with the relevant authorities.

What about road access and utilities?

A short road link connects the site to the Agra-Lucknow Expressway. Power, water and other utilities would be confirmed as part of the proposal.

Who are you talking to?

Companies across the aviation industry, to understand what they need, and the relevant authorities, to understand the rules. We have not announced any partners.
