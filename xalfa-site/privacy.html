Privacy

How we use your details

What we collect

Only the details you choose to send us: by email, or through the contact form on our website. The form does not send anything itself. It opens your own email app with your message ready, and you decide whether to send it.

What we use it for

To reply to you, and to keep a record of the conversation.

What we do not do

We do not sell or share your details. Our website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers.
